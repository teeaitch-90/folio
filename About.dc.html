A little about me Everyone in my family went into healthcare. I went into design, which turned out to be a pretty similar job. Listen properly, figure out what's actually going on, fix that. I just liked the making part more - where one good decision can quietly make the day better for thousands of people. And every so often someone says "oh, that's clever." Still gets me. I have been around different industries from tech, finance, healthcare, banking apps, enterprise tooling and design systems. Different rooms, same job. Start where the customer actually is, work backwards, ship something that holds up. Usually wearing a few hats along the way, running the project as much as designing it. I've seen design shift a few times over twelve years. Each time, the answer was the same: stay close to the customer, be deliberate about what's worth building, and treat AI as something that should make the product better, not just newer.
